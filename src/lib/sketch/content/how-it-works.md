1. Users post naturally on Bluesky, Blacksky or any other way. No special app required.
1. Labeler services label posts just as they do now. 
1. Labelers are configured with an **action URL** that provides context or services relevant to the labeled content
1. Clients (like Skyline) show labeled posts with badges. Clicking a badge goes to the labeler's action URL, passing the content ID, and maybe the label value
1. The labeler provides a page relevant to the specific content.
