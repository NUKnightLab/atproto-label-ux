A **UX sketch** in this sense is a prototype meant to communicate a design concept — not to be production-ready or fully implemented.

The sketch system provides three tools:

1. **The bar and drawer** — A fixed top bar labels the sketch. Clicking it opens a side panel with context, explanations, and per-page notes written in markdown.

2. **Design annotations** — Dashed callout boxes that explain design intent inline, visible to anyone reviewing the prototype.

3. **Wiggle feedback** — Unimplemented UI elements (buttons, dead links) wiggle when clicked, signaling "this isn't wired up yet" without breaking the illusion of a working interface.
